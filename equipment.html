Brewing Equipment

Six tried-and-tested tools for better coffee at home, each with detailed descriptions and practical usage tips from our brew bar team.

Six essential tools

Choose your brew kit

Every item is used daily at our brew bar, so the advice below comes from real experience.

Barista Pro Espresso Machine

A compact semi-automatic machine with a 58 mm portafilter, PID temperature control and a built-in steam wand — the biggest single upgrade most home baristas can make.

Usage tips
  • Always preheat the group head and cups for 5 minutes.
  • Dose 18 g of coffee and aim for a 36 g shot in 28 seconds.
  • Purge the steam wand briefly before and after frothing.
£29.50

Classic Glass French Press

A 1 litre borosilicate press with a stainless steel mesh filter. Full-immersion brewing gives a rich, full-bodied cup with no paper filters and no waste.

Usage tips
  • Use a coarse, even grind — like coarse sea salt.
  • Steep for 4 minutes, break the crust, then skim and press gently.
  • Decant every drop so the coffee does not keep brewing.
£34.00

Ceramic Pour-over Set

Cone dripper, 600 ml carafe and 50 filter papers in one box. The ribbed cone keeps the paper off the glass so the coffee flows evenly and cleanly.

Usage tips
  • Rinse the paper first to remove any papery taste.
  • Bloom with twice the coffee weight in water for 30 seconds.
  • Pour slowly in circles; finish the brew within 3 minutes.
£89.00

Conical Burr Coffee Grinder

Forty stainless steel settings from Turkish-fine to cold-brew coarse, with a timed dosing hopper. Consistent particles are the cheapest way to improve any brew.

Usage tips
  • Adjust the grind one step at a time — never change two variables at once.
  • Grind fresh, right before brewing, for the best aroma.
  • Brush the burrs weekly so old grounds do not turn rancid.
£59.00

Precision Gooseneck Kettle

A 900 ml variable-temperature kettle with a slim spout for total control of flow rate — essential for pour-over coffee and tea alike.

Usage tips
  • Brew filter coffee at 92–96 °C, not rolling boil.
  • Keep the spout low and pour in slow, steady circles.
  • Use the hold button to keep temperature stable while brewing.
£29.95

AeroPress Travel Brewer

Nearly unbreakable, endlessly forgiving and ready in a minute. The favourite of campers, office workers and anyone who wants great coffee away from home.

Usage tips
  • Try the inverted method for a longer, sweeter steep.
  • Use a medium-fine grind and a 1:12 coffee-to-water ratio.
  • Press slowly for 30 seconds — never force the plunger.
